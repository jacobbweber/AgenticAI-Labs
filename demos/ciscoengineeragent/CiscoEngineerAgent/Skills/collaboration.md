# Collaboration

**Load when:** Webex, CUCM, Jabber, calling, contact center, UCCX, phone, Webex Calling

## When to load
Calling, meetings, contact center, or PSTN migration (on-prem CUCM vs Webex Calling).

## What the SE must produce
1. User counts, PSTN model, analog leftovers, recording/compliance.
2. HLD: control plane, media path, survivability, identity.
3. Migration waves and coexistence (not a big-bang cutover by default).
4. PoC plan before promising audio quality.

## Multi-vendor notes
Zoom, Teams, Avaya, Mitel stay in the picture unless the RFP says rip-and-replace. Document SIP trunks and recording taps.

## Never invent SKUs
No phone PIDs, A-La-Carte licenses, or Webex SKU letters. `[UNVERIFIED]` on every license line. Confirm in CCW/partner.
